Scholar's Handbook

The Quest Map

Follow the path to master the Quran's language. Each node represents a Surah. Use IlmCoins earned in previous levels to unlock new areas!

Checkpoint Exams

Every 3 Surahs, you face a Checkpoint Exam (75% to pass). This is mandatory to unlock the next block of levels. Passing gives you 100 coins!

Earning Coins (Scholarships)

Need more coins? Click the Earn button to enter high-stakes Scholarship Exams. Enter with a fee and pass with 80% to win up to 800 coins!

Spaced Repetition (SRS)

The app is smart! When you mark a word as:

  • Hard: You see it again tomorrow.
  • Good: You see it in 3-5 days.
  • Easy: You see it in 10+ days.

The Adaptive Selector prioritizes your Hard and Due words in every session.

Streaks & Cards
